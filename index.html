Biography & Profile

About Prof. Dr. Prakash Divakaran

Academic Vision & Institutional Governance

Prof. Dr. Prakash Divakaran currently serves as Vice Chancellor at Himalayan University, Itanagar, Arunachal Pradesh. With prior administrative roles as Pro Vice Chancellor, Director, Professor, and CEO, he brings a unique multi-dimensional leadership ethos aligned with NEP 2020.

"To build globally respected, research-driven, and student-centric higher education institutions that integrate academic rigor, employability, ethics, and technological advancement."

He holds double doctoral qualifications in Business Administration and Management, complemented by extensive corporate experience in global Supply Chain Management with automotive giants.

Key Strengths & Impact Highlights

  • University Governance: Driving UGC compliance, NAAC/NBA accreditation readiness, outcome-based curricula, and IQAC processes.
  • Research Ecosystem: Editor-in-Chief of Scopus-aligned & UGC journals; successfully mentored 14+ awarded Ph.D. candidates.
  • International Exposure: Chaired academic forums across USA, Singapore, UAE, Malaysia, Thailand, Philippines, and Vietnam.
  • Rare Industry Integration: Blending 15+ years of corporate SCM leadership (Polyrub, Minda, TATA Yazaki) into practical curriculum design.
